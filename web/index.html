What this is

Grid Desk watches Australia's National Electricity Market (NEM) the way an operations desk would: demand, prices, generation and interconnector flows every 5 minutes, AEMO's reserve notices, and units dropping out. For each region it learns what demand normally looks like for the time of day, day of the week, temperature and rooftop sunshine, and says when today departs from that, and why.

The data-centre tab shows how much electricity data centres use and plan to use, as honestly as public data allows. It keeps three kinds of number apart: published (from AEMO's reports), announced (operators' stated capacity, a maximum) and estimated (our own inference, always with a range). It never claims to measure one facility's live use and never blames a facility for a grid event.

It is an independent project, not affiliated with AEMO or any operator, and nothing here is trading or investment advice.
